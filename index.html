How to use the worksheets

  1. Choose a letter worksheet (PDF).
  2. Print it on A4 paper.
  3. Play the matching YouTube writing lesson.
  4. Let your child trace ➜ write ➜ repeat.

Best for ages 3+ and beginner Bangla learners.

Why Zoya TV Bangla worksheets?

Every sheet is designed to match a video, so kids can see, hear, and write at the same time – making learning smooth and fun.

Kid-friendly design
